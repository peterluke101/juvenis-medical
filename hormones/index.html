Every hormone protocol at Juvenis Medical begins with a comprehensive panel — 70+ factors, $100 to $345, no insurance required. Dr. Paul reviews the results with you personally before anything is prescribed.

What a hormone panel typically covers

  • Total and free testosterone
  • Estradiol (E2) and SHBG
  • LH and FSH
  • Progesterone and DHEA-S
  • TSH, free T3, free T4, reverse T3
  • Full metabolic panel and CBC
  • Lipids and inflammatory markers
  • PSA where clinically appropriate
Where we can treat you: hormone therapy — TRT, women's hormones, and thyroid — is available by telehealth to Florida residents only. In-person care at our Oakland Park clinic is open to patients from any state. Non-controlled peptides remain available nationwide.
