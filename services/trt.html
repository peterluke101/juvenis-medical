Why It Matters

Testosterone Peaks at 22.
Then It Declines.

After your mid-twenties, testosterone levels drop about 1–2% per year. By the time most men notice — fatigue, reduced drive, weight gain, mood changes — levels may have already fallen significantly. The good news: it's measurable, treatable, and reversible.

Energy & Vitality

Low testosterone is one of the most common causes of unexplained fatigue and lack of motivation. Optimized levels restore the drive to perform at your best.

Sexual Health & Libido

Testosterone plays a central role in sexual function and desire. TRT can meaningfully improve libido, performance, and overall satisfaction.

Mood & Cognition

Brain fog, irritability, and difficulty concentrating are frequently linked to hormonal imbalance. Many patients report dramatic improvements in mental clarity.

Muscle & Bone Density

Testosterone supports lean muscle mass and bone strength. Optimized levels help you build muscle more effectively and reduce long-term fracture risk.

Cardiovascular Health

Research links healthy testosterone levels to better cardiovascular markers including improved lipid profiles and reduced inflammatory markers.

Body Composition

Low testosterone encourages fat storage, particularly around the abdomen. TRT combined with proper lifestyle habits helps shift body composition in the right direction.

Fertility & Testicular Function

HCG or Enclomiphene —
Depending on Your Labs

Testosterone taken from the outside tells the brain to stop asking for its own, which is why testicular size and fertility are a real consideration on TRT rather than a footnote. Dr. Paul addresses this directly, using HCG or enclomiphene depending on your lab work, your history, and what you want from treatment.

HCG

Mimics LH — the signal your pituitary would normally send — so the testes keep working while you are on testosterone. Prescribed alongside TRT where it fits the picture.

Enclomiphene

Works further upstream, prompting your own testosterone production rather than replacing it. For some men it is the right tool alongside TRT; for others it is an alternative to it.

Which One, and When

There is no default here. The choice comes out of your bloodwork, your fertility plans, and your goals — and it is revisited at every follow-up panel.

Estradiol Management

Some testosterone converts to estradiol, and the ratio matters as much as the number. E2 is measured on your panel and managed as part of the protocol.

Worth raising early: if fertility now or later is part of your thinking, say so on the first call. It changes how a protocol is built, and it is far easier to plan for at the start than to correct later.

Why it is on a men's panel
