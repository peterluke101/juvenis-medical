Bioidentical Hormones for Women:
Estrogen, Progesterone & Testosterone

Bioidentical means the molecule matches what your body already makes. That is the whole definition. It is not a brand, a guarantee, or a menu of products. At Juvenis Medical, BHRT is labs-first: a 70+ factor panel, then a protocol if you are a candidate.

Start care on Women's BHRT. The midlife map is the menopause guide.

What Bioidentical Means

Simply put: bioidentical hormones are molecularly identical to the hormones your body produces. They are compounded to match your labs. That is different from a one-size oral prescription written without a full panel.

It is also not a promise that everyone is a candidate. The evaluation screens first.

The Three Hormones We Discuss Most

Bi-Est (topical / transdermal)

Estrogen here is topical and transdermal only. The usual prescription is Bi-Est rather than estradiol as the standard starting prescription. There is no oral or vaginal menu. Bioidentical progesterone is prescribed with it — for bone, mood, and sleep, not only endometrial protection.

Progesterone

Discussed for balance with estrogen, sleep, and mood. Often part of the plan if you still have a uterus. Beneficial for many others — decided clinically, not on a blog.

Testosterone

Women produce testosterone. Low levels can sit under libido, energy, and body-composition complaints. This is a low-dose female protocol, not mini-TRT.
