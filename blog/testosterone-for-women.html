Testosterone for Women:
It's Not Just a Men's Issue

Women produce testosterone. When it is low, libido, energy, and body composition can all feel off — even if estradiol and progesterone have already been discussed. This is a low-dose, lab-guided conversation inside BHRT. It is not mini-TRT.

The Women's BHRT page already says a small, properly dosed amount can matter. The midlife map is the menopause care guide.

Women Make Testosterone Too

Testosterone in women is not a trend. It is a hormone you already make. Levels can fall in perimenopause and menopause. Symptoms overlap with estrogen and thyroid stories, which is why we do not treat a single number in isolation.

Common reasons it comes up: lower desire, flatter energy, harder body composition despite the same habits. Those are reasons to test — not reasons to start a men’s protocol.

Not Mini-TRT

Men’s TRT doses and goals do not transfer. A female protocol, when it is appropriate, is a different dose range, a different monitoring conversation, and it usually sits next to estradiol and progesterone — not instead of them. See bioidentical hormones for women.
